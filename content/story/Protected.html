All protected and conserved areas

Based on our current knowledge, 86 percent of the 438 native vascular plant species known to the island have been reported within its protected and conserved areas, from provincial parks and its ecological reserve to the lands held by the Galiano Conservancy Association, the Islands Trust Conservancy, the Galiano Club and other conservancies. The other 63 have so far been recorded only outside them.

This preliminary evaluation is limited by biases in sampling effort and other gaps in the data, but it is a starting point for a more thorough assessment of progress toward conserving the region’s plant diversity. Help improve it by reporting your observations to the Biodiversity Galiano Island iNaturalist project.

The map shows protected and conserved areas by category. Galiano Island has as yet no published Key Biodiversity Area, but two are proposed - Mount Sutil & Mount Galiano Bluffs, and Active Pass - and since their boundaries are not yet public, each is marked by a ring at its point. Click an area on the map, or in the chart below it, to show only the plants recorded there and its summary above, or a category in the map’s legend or the chart to show those recorded in all its areas. Choose Unprotected native plant diversity in the chart to show, as a grid on the map, where the native plants not yet reported in any protected area have been recorded. Protected areas are from the Canadian Protected and Conserved Areas Database (Environment and Climate Change Canada), and Key Biodiversity Areas from KBA Canada (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
