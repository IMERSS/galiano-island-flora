Community contributions to our knowledge of the regional plant diversity

Through the combined efforts of 752 individuals, the Galiano naturalist community has now documented a total of 812 vascular plant species. Since 2000, 83 percent of the historically reported plant diversity has been confirmed and 207 new species reports added to the list.

Community members have now documented 85 percent of the total plant diversity known to the region on iNaturalist.

Contribute to this growing record by submitting your observations to the Biodiversity Galiano Island iNaturalist project.
