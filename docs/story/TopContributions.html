Community contributions to our knowledge of the island’s plant diversity

Through the combined efforts of 767 individuals, the Galiano naturalist community has now documented a total of 812 vascular plant species. Since 2004, 83 percent of the historically reported plant diversity has been confirmed and 207 new species reports added to the list.

Through iNaturalist alone, community members have now documented 85 percent of the plant diversity known to the island.

Contribute to this growing record by submitting your observations to the Biodiversity Galiano Island iNaturalist project.
